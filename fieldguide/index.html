Oulanka Virtual Field Guide

Walk the Oulankajoki valley in 360°: from the research station along the river to the Kiutaköngäs rapids and the Puukkosuo fen. Glowing points show the public sensor stations measuring the site, and the map shows where you are and which way you are looking.

Drag to look around · arrows to walk · works on phones and computers
